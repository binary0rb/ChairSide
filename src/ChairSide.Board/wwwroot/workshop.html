Where future scheduling tools will live

Workshop is where future schedule replay, projection, and schedule-fit tools will live. These tools will use historical ChairSide timing data to model possible scheduling tradeoffs without changing live records or production behavior.

Everything below is planned. Each tool is a projection that explores scenarios and soft suggestions only — nothing here changes the live board, reporting, or production data.

Projection presets

Each preset is a planned projection lens describing a tradeoff to explore. None of them change the live board, and none assume observed slack can be automatically reclaimed.

Balanced Flow

Planned

Explore schedule shapes that keep a day steady — neither rushed nor padded. A projection lens for weighing evenness against pace, offered as options to consider rather than targets to hit.

More Throughput

Planned

Explore whether repeatable patterns might open room for additional appointments. This is a tradeoff lens, not a promise of capacity: observed slack is not automatically free time, and any change stays a scheduling decision for the team.

More Recovery

Planned

Explore whether deliberately protected time could ease late-day catch-up and schedule debt. A scenario for choosing recovery on purpose, weighed against pace — not a recommendation.

Patient Experience Protected

Planned

Explore schedule shapes that prioritize predictable, unhurried visits, protecting time around longer or sedation cases. A projection lens for a calmer-paced day, offered as a tradeoff to weigh.

Reset/Admin Buffer Protected

Planned

Explore schedule shapes that preserve turnover, reset, and admin time between cases instead of absorbing it. This protects the behind-the-scenes time a smooth day depends on.

Planned tools

Projection Sandbox / Schedule Replay

Planned

Replay historical timing data under different schedule assumptions, so you can explore how a day might have unfolded under a different plan. Every run is a projection, never a change to live records.

Case-Mix Insights

Planned

Identify procedure combinations that tend to create schedule debt or flow friction, so case-mix can be planned with those patterns in mind. Descriptive context, not a verdict on any doctor or day.

Similar-Day Comparison

Planned

Compare a day against prior days with similar doctor, procedure, sedation, and volume patterns. A like-for-like reference that keeps projections fair and grounded.
