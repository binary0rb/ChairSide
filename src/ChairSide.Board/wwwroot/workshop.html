Everything below is planned. Each tool is a projection that explores scenarios and soft suggestions only — nothing here changes the live board, reporting, or production data.

Projection presets

Each preset is a planned projection lens describing a tradeoff to explore. None of them change the live board, and none assume observed slack can be automatically reclaimed.

Balanced Flow

Planned

Explore schedule shapes that keep a day steady — neither rushed nor padded. A projection lens for weighing evenness against pace, offered as options to consider rather than targets to hit.

Balanced Flow would explore schedule shapes that keep a day steady — neither rushed nor padded. It is a lens for weighing evenness against pace: where might small spacing changes reduce friction without padding the day? Anything surfaced would be options to consider, never targets to hit.
This lens would need assumptions about how much unevenness the team is willing to smooth without padding the day.

More Throughput

Planned

Explore whether repeatable patterns might open room for additional appointments. This is a tradeoff lens, not a promise of capacity: observed slack is not automatically free time, and any change stays a scheduling decision for the team.

More Throughput would explore whether repeatable patterns in past days might leave room for additional appointments. It is a tradeoff lens, not a promise of capacity — observed slack is not automatically free time, and anything surfaced stays a scheduling decision for the team to weigh.
This lens would need an explicit, conservative assumption about whether any observed slack is usable. It must never treat all raw slack as free appointment time.

More Recovery

Planned

Explore whether deliberately protected time could ease late-day catch-up and schedule debt. A scenario for choosing recovery on purpose, weighed against pace — not a recommendation.

More Recovery would explore whether deliberately protected time could ease late-day catch-up and schedule debt. It is a scenario for choosing recovery on purpose and weighing it against pace — a description of a tradeoff, not a recommendation to follow.
This lens would need assumptions about how much protected catch-up or recovery time the team wants to preserve, and where that time can safely sit.

Patient Experience Protected

Planned

Explore schedule shapes that prioritize predictable, unhurried visits, protecting time around longer or sedation cases. A projection lens for a calmer-paced day, offered as a tradeoff to weigh.

Patient Experience Protected would explore schedule shapes that prioritize predictable, unhurried visits, protecting time around longer or sedation cases. It is a lens for picturing a calmer-paced day and what it would trade against — offered to weigh, not to adopt.
This lens would need assumptions about which visit types need extra breathing room, especially longer or sedation cases.

Reset/Admin Buffer Protected

Planned

Explore schedule shapes that preserve turnover, reset, and admin time between cases instead of absorbing it. This protects the behind-the-scenes time a smooth day depends on.

Reset/Admin Buffer Protected would explore schedule shapes that preserve turnover, reset, and admin time between cases instead of absorbing it. It is a lens for protecting the behind-the-scenes time a smooth day depends on, and seeing what that protection would cost elsewhere.
This lens would need assumptions about turnover, reset, documentation, and admin time that should be protected instead of absorbed.

Planned tools

Projection Sandbox / Schedule Replay

Planned

Replay historical timing data under different schedule assumptions, so you can explore how a day might have unfolded under a different plan. Every run is a projection, never a change to live records.

Case-Mix Insights

Planned

Identify procedure combinations that tend to create schedule debt or flow friction, so case-mix can be planned with those patterns in mind. Descriptive context, not a verdict on any doctor or day.

Similar-Day Comparison

Planned

Compare a day against prior days with similar doctor, procedure, sedation, and volume patterns. A like-for-like reference that keeps projections fair and grounded.
